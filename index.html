Beijing Institute of Technology · Number theory

Yangyu Fan 范洋宇

I study number theory, with a focus on p-adic methods, automorphic representations, and arithmetic aspects of automorphic forms.

My work concerns periods and branching, families of representations, Euler systems, and p-adic L-functions. I am also interested in research on AI safety.

About & contact

I am a pre-tenure assistant professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Beijing Institute of Technology.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Milan in 2018, after studying at Sichuan University and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. Before joining BIT, I held postdoctoral positions at the Morningside Center of Mathematics and Capital Normal University.
