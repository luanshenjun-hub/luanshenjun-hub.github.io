About

Number theory, p-adic methods, automorphic representations, and arithmetic geometry.

My research is in number theory, with a focus on the representation theory of p-adic groups and arithmetic aspects of automorphic forms.

I am a pre-tenure assistant professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Beijing Institute of Technology. My work concerns Ext-groups, spherical varieties, periods, and families of representations, together with related questions involving Euler systems and p-adic L-functions.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Milan in 2018, after studying at Sichuan University and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. Before joining BIT, I held postdoctoral positions at the Morningside Center of Mathematics and Capital Normal University.
