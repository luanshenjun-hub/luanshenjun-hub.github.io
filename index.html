About

Number theory, p-adic methods, and automorphic phenomena.

I study arithmetic through the interplay of p-adic analysis, representation theory, and geometry.

I am a pre-tenure assistant professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at Beijing Institute of Technology. My research focuses on p-adic special-value formulas and their arithmetic applications, together with questions about representations of p-adic groups, local periods, and families.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Milan in 2018, after studying at Sichuan University and the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. Before joining BIT, I held postdoctoral positions at the Morningside Center of Mathematics and Capital Normal University.
